| `ae-entry-gate` | Apartment Events | anonymous | `auth-entry-gate` | Fresh entry gate before any account exists | docs/Build Plan V2/Evidence/B25/phase-a-apartment-events/screenshots/entry-gate.png | `ba60ac24b4c0925f197c4e4a0d9da1572f0147c0e5443727a866132b2e0a4900` | fail |  | LLM vision critique: The card copy "Welcome to Loom" / "Choose an account below or create a new one." is accurate for the general gate, but the "Existing Accounts" heading sits above roughly 100px of pure whitespace terminated by a hairline divider, with no "No accounts yet" or "Sign up to get started" text. On a fresh gate that blank strip is indistinguishable from a list that is still loading or that failed to load, so state legibility is ambiguous at the exact moment it matters most. Community context is also demoted: the only mention of "Apartment Events" is in the bottom footer sentence "Choose an active account or create one to continue to Apartment Events." while the hero says "Welcome to Loom" — a first-time user lands on a screen whose largest text names the platform rather than the community they were invited to. Positively, the "Persona type" dropdown is pre-populated with a real community role ("Event Manager"), not a generic placeholder, and the filled teal "Sign Up" button plus outlined "Check membership status" button give a clear primary/secondary hierarchy. |
| `ae-home-role-summary` | Apartment Events | apartment-event-manager (Jamie_Lee) | `community-home` | Home tab immediately after signing up as Event Manager | docs/Build Plan V2/Evidence/B25/phase-a-apartment-events/screenshots/home-role-summary.png | `0127138161cc5954ae6570162ecef23a07cfc1ec8338ca7fc58afa7a379dcd5d` | fail |  | LLM vision critique: Immediately after signing up as Jamie_Lee, the Home tab shows no trace of Jamie_Lee. The app bar carries only "Apartment Events" and three icons (bell, speech bubble, people); there is no avatar, initial, or name anywhere on the screen. The teal welcome card correctly states the community identity and tagline ("Classes, gatherings, and amenity reservations for neighbors.") and the nested role tile is accurate product copy ("Event Manager / Apartment Event Manager - Creates, edits, and cancels resident events and publishes reservable facility windows."), but the pill to its right reads only "3 roles" with no indication of what those three are, whether the user holds all three, or that the pill is even tappable — it is styled as a flat translucent chip, not a button. Two copy problems: "No sponsored message right now." is a system-status sentence exposed to end users where a real product would simply render nothing, and "Home — Pinned and unassigned community surfaces." is internal architecture vocabulary ("unassigned surfaces") leaking into user-facing copy. The empty state below ("Nothing is pinned yet" / "Apartment Events does not have Home…") is the right idea but is sliced off mid-sentence by the bottom nav bar, so its explanation is unreadable at rest. Contrast is otherwise sound: white text on dark teal, and the selected "Home" nav pill is clearly distinguished from "Calendar" and "Messages". |

The grouping-by-persona pattern is genuinely good and the ID line disambiguates same-named accounts. The problems are framing and state. Although this screen is reached from "Sign in as a specific person" inside the role dialog, it renders the generic entry-gate hero — "Welcome to Loom" / "Choose an account below or create a new one." — rather than a title matching the action taken, and it drops all community context: "Apartment Events" appears nowhere, and unlike the entry gate (screen 1) there is no footer band saying which community you are continuing to. Critically, the Jamie_Lee row carries no "currently signed in" / "active" marker, no checkmark, and no visual distinction, so the user cannot tell from pixels that this is the session they are already in versus a different account they would be switching to — the row looks purely like a sign-in target. The full "Create New Account" form (empty "Display name", "Persona type: Event Manager", filled "Sign Up") is also carried over verbatim, which is odd for a screen entered from a role dialog to pick a person. There is no visible back arrow, close control, or cancel affordance to return to the dialog. |
